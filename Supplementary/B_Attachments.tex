        \centering
        \includegraphics[width=14cm]{"SUP4.3.3_helices"}
\caption{
\textbf{Examplary plots of α-helices of the uTPs studies in this work}. 
    The three \gls{utp}s (\gls{utp_B}, \gls{utp_E}, \gls{utp_C}) were analyzed with HeliQuest (\cite{gautier_heliquest_2008}) which plotted these helices. Arrow symbolized hydrophobic moment and pointed to the hydrophobic face. Residues are coloured by characteristics (blue for basic, red for acidic, purple/pink for polar, yellow/gray for hydrophobic residues).}
       \label{sup:helices}
